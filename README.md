📊 Sales Insights Dashboard
🧾 Overview



This Excel dashboard provides an analytical overview of Sales Insights for a retail business. It helps understand sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends through interactive visuals and KPIs.

📈 Key Metrics
Gross Percentage: 4761.90
Gross Income: 15379.37
Average Rating by Product Line
📊 Visuals Included
3D Bar Chart – Comparison of Gross Margin Percentage and Gross Income by Branch.
Line Chart – Trend of Gross Income over time.
Bar Chart (Category-wise) – Distribution of Gross Income across Product Lines.
Horizontal Bar Chart – Average Rating by Product Line.
Customer Type Analysis – Gross Income by Customer Type (Member vs Normal).
KPI Cards – Highlight overall Gross Percentage and Gross Income.
Slicers/Filters – For Branch, City, Product Line, Date, and Customer Type.
🧠 Insights
Branch C shows the highest gross margin and income performance.
Food and Beverages and Fashion Accessories are top-performing categories.
Members contribute more to gross income compared to normal customers.
Sales and income show consistent growth patterns across time.
🏙️ Filters Used
Branch: A, B, C
City: Naypyitaw, Mandalay, Yangon
Product Line: 6 major categories
Date: Time-based slicer (Year/Quarter view)
🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI Desktop
Dataset: Retail Sales Dataset (CSV/Excel)
Data Cleaning: Power Query Editor
Visualization: Power BI Charts, Cards, and Slicers
💡 Purpose



The dashboard was created as part of a data analysis project to:

Visualize sales performance across branches and categories.
Identify key profit drivers and customer patterns.
Provide actionable business insights.
